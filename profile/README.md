
### Who We Are
**Uktam.ai** is dedicated to democratizing AI access across India by bringing state-of-the-art Indic linguistic models directly to edge devices. We build technology that breaks down language barriers in real-time—without compromising on privacy, speed, or connectivity.

By leveraging and heavily optimizing foundational models from leading Indian AI research labs like **Sarvam AI** and **AI4Bharat**, we ensure our technology accurately captures the rich nuances, dialects, and grammatical complexities of Indic languages.

By completely eliminating the need for cloud APIs, our solutions run perfectly in remote areas, low-bandwidth environments, and high-security contexts where data privacy is paramount.
